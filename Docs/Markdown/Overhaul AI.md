# Tuning Overhaul AI

The host can tune NPCs live under **F9 → Overhaul AI**. A level author can also tune each fighter in the character editor. Overhaul responds to all three original difficulty presets. The lobby's Difficulty Scaling mod tracks encounter pressure separately, and its actor-level changes currently skip standard Overhaul fighters.

## What a fighter does

The character still chooses goals such as investigating, fighting or fleeing. In a fight, Overhaul adjusts the choice to rush, provoke or surround, then evaluates the current target for air maneuvers, throws, flanking, terrain, individual combat and team support. Different opponents can respond differently to the same player move because their style, gear, allies and recent observations affect which actions qualify. [Goal selection](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/enemycontrol.as#L1212) · [Combat update](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L135)

- **Spacing and rhythm.** Footwork can angle in, feint an approach or retreat to draw a swing. Tempo can schedule a 0.4–0.9 second pause followed by a 0.8–1.4 second rush. Attacking during the pause can end it early. Close-combat sense checks for openings such as a whiff, an exposed back or a swing aimed at somebody else. [Footwork](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_mind.as#L867) · [Tempo](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_mind.as#L378) · [Opening response](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_combat.as#L145)
- **Reading the player.** The fighter tracks blocks, reversals and catches against individual opponents. Those counts lose 15% every ten game seconds. **Read the opponent** lets the fighter change its mix of attacks, feints and grabs. Repeated flying kicks have their own learning path. [Opponent memory](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_mind.as#L227) · [Learning from kicks](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L1092)
- **Defense.** An active block needs an eligible incoming attack, an available defense and an animation cue. The timing roll uses authored Block Skill, how predictable the attack is, the fighter's subgoal, follower status and base difficulty. A failed roll adds 0.4 seconds to the response, which can let the hit land. [Defense eligibility and block timing](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/enemycontrol.as#L2410)
- **Group pressure.** With Group tactics on, fighters count allies already committed to the same target. The usual allowance is one striker on Casual and two on Hardcore or Expert. A team pressing its advantage can add one. Others flank or occupy the target's attention. A nearby fighter can jump the queue when the target swings at someone else. [Striker allocation and exceptions](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L4062)

## Controls you can use today

The four F9 sliders run from **0 to 2×** and default to **1×**. Each scales the fighter's authored value before personality and situational checks. These values control how readily an opportunity is taken. A value of 0.5 does not mean half of all attacks will use the move. [F9 controls](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/overhaul_tab_ai.as#L18) · [Settings calculation](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L50)

- **Jump kicks** (`aio_jump_kicks`, authored default 0.5) affects offensive flying kicks, including opening, pursuit and close-range kicks. Rabbits, cats and rats can initiate them. [Species check](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L251)
- **Air combat** (`aio_air_combat`, 0.5) affects responses to an airborne opponent, including evasion, interception and landing punishment. For a test of repeated player jump kicks, start here and leave Jump kicks at 1× to keep ordinary offensive kick frequency comparable. [Interception](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L860) · [Landing response](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L938)
- **Weapon throwing** (`aio_throwing`, 0.5) affects attempts with suitable weapons, including throws at exposed or fleeing targets. Weapon sense also considers the value of keeping the weapon and whether the opponent catches throws. [Throw selection](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L3744)
- **Feints** (`aio_feints`, 0.3) affects attempts to draw a premature block. Read the opponent and personality can further change the decision. [Feint decision](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L3955)

**Individual and team switches.** F9 exposes Close-combat sense, Weapon sense, Fighting personalities, Read the opponent, Unpredictable footwork, Change the rhythm and Catch their breath. Group tactics controls flanking and striker allocation. Team tactics, Stick together and Morale control coordination and retreat. Movement options separately enable rolls, fast get-ups, fake retreats, wall maneuvers and ledge use. The debug section can display NPC goals and stealth cues or write individual decisions to the log with the `[AIO]` prefix. [Switches and debug options](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/overhaul_tab_ai.as#L19)

**Per fighter.** The editor's four `AI -` sliders run from 0 to 1. A fighter can opt out of Overhaul, group or team tactics. `AI - Style` accepts auto, balanced, kicker, boxer, trickster, brawler or skirmisher. Authored Aggression, Ground Aggression, Block Skill, Block Follow-up, movement and attack speed, damage, resistance and weapon-catching skill are additional controls. Focus and peripheral sight distances and the optional Hearing Modifier govern detection. [Character parameters](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aschar.as#L16308) · [Overhaul parameters](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aschar.as#L16470) · [Hearing](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/enemycontrol.as#L1094)

## Which difficulty settings reach Overhaul?

- **Casual, Hardcore and Expert all work.** Their combat difficulty values are 0, 0.5 and 1. The Game Difficulty slider supplies intermediate values. Overhaul reads this value live. Chase prediction looks back 150, 95 or 40 milliseconds respectively. An eligible aerial counter starts with a difficulty coefficient of 0.25, 0.50 or 0.75 before air skill and learned behavior. Feints use 0.10, 0.275 or 0.45 before their other multipliers. Group size changes at 0.5, so Hardcore and Expert share the ordinary striker allowance. The preset's game speed, tutorial and automatic ledge-grab settings retain their separate functions. [Preset definitions](../../Source/Internal/config.cpp#L364) · [Live difficulty read](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aschar.as#L2830) · [Chase prediction](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/enemycontrol.as#L3183)
- **Active time, level or match completion, and performance** feed the host's encounter pressure when enabled. Off, Gentle, Standard, Brisk and Nightmare are rates for the relevant scaling controls. Choosing Nightmare for AI scaling does not select a fourth Overhaul combat preset. In a solo lobby on `fight_test.xml` with both mods active, completing an encounter added 4% pressure with the tested settings. The same live Overhaul fighter also changed from 0.5 to 1 when the host selected Expert. [Lobby settings](../../Data/Scripts/settings.as#L681) · [Difficulty Scaling rules](Campaign%20Difficulty.md)
- **AI scaling, party stats and reinforcements, physical armor, weapons for extra enemies, and scaled perception** require the fork's character integration. Overhaul supplies its own character, enemy-control and awareness scripts. Its standard fighter lacks `SetCampaignScaling(float, float, float, float)` and the companion armor and perception fields. The host skips it when applying native stat changes, selecting reinforcement sources or generating equipment. Authored and Overhaul-assigned equipment still work. [Eligibility](../../Source/Online/campaign_difficulty.cpp#L104) · [Equipment](../../Source/Online/campaign_difficulty.cpp#L798) · [Character stat delivery](../../Source/Online/campaign_difficulty.cpp#L1214)
- **Friendly Progression** also needs its own integration. The engine checks for `progression_ai` before applying the slot's purchased gear, stats and bot AI tier. The Overhaul character lacks that field and the fork's hit-XP reporting. Its sight and hearing paths also lack the fork's passive and crouched stealth factors. The lobby can retain a purchase while the character never receives its benefit. [Progression delivery](../../Source/Online/campaign_difficulty.cpp#L2141) · [Overhaul vision and hearing](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/enemycontrol.as#L1094)
- **Large Battles → Team → Skill** is Overhaul's own 0–1 spawn control. It sets block skill and follow-up, attack speed, damage and knockback, movement speed, aggression, ground aggression and damage resistance. Toughness, equipment and the team's AI mode are additional independent choices. [Battle fighter setup](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_battle.as#L937)

## Where to connect dynamic difficulty

For a first integration, snapshot host pressure at the existing load, checkpoint, retry or new-match boundary. Use that snapshot to vary **air-counter readiness, feint tendency and willingness to punish a missed attack**. Those decisions each have an observable outcome and can be tested independently. Group pressure has two matching striker-cap calculations that should use the same sampled value. [Encounter sampling](Campaign%20Difficulty.md) · [Team cap](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L3173) · [Group cap](../../mod/overgrowth-online/Data/Scripts/aio_brain.as#L4098)

For the four continuous drives, compose the sampled per-fighter multiplier in `AIORefreshSettings()` after it reconstructs the authored value × host slider and before it applies personality. This preserves authored zeros and host switches, avoids multiplying an already-scaled value on every refresh, and lets an ongoing fight retain the pressure it started with. The fork's existing AI variation selects only about one in eight eligible enemies. An encounter-wide difficulty curve needs access to the sampled pressure itself. Restoring armor, senses, purchased upgrades and hit XP requires their respective character hooks as well.
